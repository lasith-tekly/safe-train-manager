Baseline Calculation

The theoretical budget allocation per quarter, weighted by the number of PI iterations in that quarter. IP iterations are excluded.

-- Baseline per quarter baseline_q = (total_budget × iters_in_q) ÷ total_iters -- Example: FM total = 4,345 KEUR, 14 total iters Q1 = 4,345 × 4/14 = 1,241 KEUR Q2 = 4,345 × 3/14 = 931 KEUR Q3 = 4,345 × 4/14 = 1,241 KEUR Q4 = 4,345 × 3/14 = 932 KEUR

Strategic Forecast (Future Quarters)

Quarters where roadmap planning is committed show actual planned KEUR. Future unplanned quarters redistribute the remaining budget by iteration ratio.

-- Past/current (committed roadmap) strategic_q = actual roadmap allocation -- Future quarters (forecast) remaining = total_budget − Σ committed_quarters rem_iters = sum of iters in unplanned quarters forecast_q = remaining × iters_in_q ÷ rem_iters
Example: Total = 14,347 K · Q1 planned = 1,936 K
Remaining = 12,411 K · rem iters Q2+Q3+Q4 = 10
Forecast Q2 = 12,411 × 3/10 = 3,723 K
Forecast Q3 = 12,411 × 4/10 = 4,964 K
Forecast Q4 = 12,411 × 3/10 = 3,723 K

Utilisation

Actual consumption as a percentage of total budget allocation for the period.

utilisation = actual ÷ total_budget × 100 -- Colour thresholds < 40% → Green (on track) 40–80% → Orange (monitor) > 80% → Red (at risk)

Quarter Classification

Quarters are classified relative to today's date to determine Past / Current / Future status.

-- Based on today's date Past → quarter_end < today Current → quarter_start ≤ today ≤ quarter_end Future → quarter_start > today -- Quarter boundaries from PI calendar -- (first iter start → last iter end per quarter)
